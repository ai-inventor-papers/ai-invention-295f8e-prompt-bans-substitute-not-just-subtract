## Full Summary

Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (bf16, rev 0e9e39f) replication of Claim 1 (does a ban remove the default and send the model to a predictable next rung?) on the frozen 12-task exp11 coding bench, plus a descriptive path-task screen against exp10's landings. Ban-free forecasts were built and sha256 hash-chained before any ban prompt was rendered (grep audit: 0 ban prompts in 10,328 prior rows): SUBTRACT (exact one-shot/persistent/iterated signature-n-gram vetoes by truncate-and-redraw in vLLM), LADDER (nonce ban, novelty instruction, 3 matched-norm random directions added at the spec positions of layers 11-21 during prefill), ASSOCIATE (Llama's own association answers) and PROXIMITY (Zhang-Shasha AST distance), in a mass-transfer form with point-mass sensitivities. Then 1,800 bans were sampled (12 modal + 6 present-minor events, W1-W5 x 20) and scored with the frozen C1 rule. Results (within Llama, pre-pooling): 13 events gated, 11 disagreeing and gated; only 2 wins (SUBTRACT on T05 via its undefined->uniform fallback; ASSOCIATE on T03 heapify_pop), so no family advances and the branch is 'NO FAMILY ADVANCES (aimed, unresolved)'. Exact Wilcoxon (n=11): SUBTRACT and LADDER beat PROXIMITY (+0.345 / +0.306 nats, p=0.0029 / 0.0020, Holm-rejected); neither separates from ASSOCIATE (p=0.52 / 0.77). Top-1 hits: LADDER and ASSOCIATE 7/13 (binomial p=0.033 vs chance 0.27), SUBTRACT 4/12, PROXIMITY 3/13. Fragile: correct-only, bf16-only decision set and drop-W4 sensitivities give SUBSTITUTION (ASSOCIATE). Five defaults (R1, R3, T13, R6, T11) are not avoided under the ban (avoidance 0.04-0.30), and surface-token vetoes rarely remove a default (only T14 heap).
