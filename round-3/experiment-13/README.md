## Layman Summary

Tests whether hints gathered without ever showing a ban can predict which algorithm a code model switches to when told not to use its usual one; mostly they cannot.

## Full Summary

Iteration-3 confirmation experiment on Qwen3-8B (bf16, rev b968826d, thinking off) over the 12 frozen bench tasks of exp11 (art_0ndRfWKBWclR). Ban-free forecasts of where each task's modal 'do not use X' ban lands were built and hash-sealed (commitments.log, SEAL_BEFORE_BANS) before any ban sample: SUBTRACT (token vetoes of the default's distinctive n-gram, coupled to neutral-twin samples by exact rejection + masked redraw via vLLM logit_bias; one-shot, persistent, 3 iterated rounds), LADDER (nonce ban, novelty instruction, 3 matched-norm isotropic random directions patched into task-spec states at layers 12-23), ASSOCIATE (exp11 association answers) and PROXIMITY (AST Zhang-Shasha distance). Then 1,200 ban samples (5 templates x 20 per task) were labelled by the gated exp11 labellers (re-check matched gate.json on 12/12). RESULTS: only 5/12 bans pass the gate (T03, T06, T05, T10, T13; R1, R3, R6, T11 keep the default almost always). The frozen C1 rule returns 'SUBTRACTION/LADDER (LADDER)' (wins LADDER 3, PROXIMITY 2), but 2 of LADDER's wins come from an undefined forecast scored as uniform; counting defined forecasts only, no family advances, and no family beats the uniform reference on average (mean log score minus uniform over gated events: LADDER +0.06, SUBTRACT -0.69, PROXIMITY -1.53, ASSOCIATE -1.78). Landings are X-specific but largely unforeseen (T06 -> index_marking, T10 -> bfs, T13 -> 'other'; only T03 quickselect and T05 brute_force were hit by a top-1). C2b is UNDETERMINED: one-shot vetoes never reach 0.5 avoidance on a gated task; descriptively the last iterated veto round lands elsewhere on T06 and T10. Token vetoes move the surface, not the algorithm: the default re-emerges in every veto round on 9/12 tasks, with text-level leaks of the vetoed n-gram in 23% (one-shot) to 54% (round 2) of samples; a sham veto breaks programs without moving the algorithm. Band-patching the ban's own task-spec states into the twin reproduces avoidance only on T03 and T10. Wording matters: template LR 374 (df 4, p ~ 1e-79; shuffled placebo p 0.39), W4 'X is not allowed here.' fails on 10/12 tasks. FRLP fp32 rescoring: median |dL_corr| 0.88 nats vs FP8, argmax unchanged 12/12.
