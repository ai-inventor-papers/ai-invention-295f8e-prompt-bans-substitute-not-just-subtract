Comparators share 50 CRN slot streams per unit: global T 1.3/1.6/2.0 with top-p 0.95, min-p 0.1 at T 3, EDT-style and AdapT-style temperature, the same dose at the highest-entropy or a random position, class-uniform forcing; boundary window arm on T1/T2/T03.
