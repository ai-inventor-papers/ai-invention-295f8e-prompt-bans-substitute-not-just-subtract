Tests what Qwen3-8B writes when told not to use its usual path-finding algorithms: with four or five names banned at once it mostly writes the banned ones anyway, often without naming them.

## Full Summary

Qwen3-8B (thinking off, vLLM 0.11, T=1.0) multi-name ban study on the iteration-1 grid-path (T1) and weighted-shortest-path (T2) suite, with mechanism-level labels (runtime tracer + AST + blind judge). The grid catalogue was extended to 10 algorithms (greedy split from A*, IDA*, JPS4, bidirectional A*) with new references (25/25 runtime and AST) and unit probes (4/4). The canonical labeller re-check gave 135/144 and 22/22. The 40-program gold gate was missed (33/40, then 32/40 after one revision; kappa 0.53), excluding unit-weight Dijkstra, JPS and bidirectional A*. KEEP RULE (>=70% of 40 forced programs labelled as requested): only BFS, DFS, IDDFS, greedy and IDA* pass, so the extension failed (F4). The analysis catalogue (K=7) adds A* and BiBFS (flagged), giving 4-name TOP/ARC/SCATTER bans plus the literal five-name TOP5. FRLP cells (about 17k teacher-forced passes: ban, mention and neutral twins, nonce, LOS, tier 3) and six landing forecasts per set (LADDER, PRIME_AND_VETO, PROXIMITY, FRLP_BAN, GENERIC, CORPUS) were sha256 hash-chained before sampling; the chain verifies and the commit precedes session 2. RESULTS (N=100 per condition, 5 templates x 20): single bans work (T1 ban BFS: DFS 86, avoidance 0.86; T2 ban Dijkstra: BF/SPFA 83, avoidance 0.84). Every multi-ban fails its gate: TOP (BFS, DFS, greedy, IDDFS) avoidance 0.13; ARC (BFS, BiBFS, A*, greedy) 0.37 (DFS 37 survivor); SCATTER (BFS, DFS, greedy, IDA*) 0.04; TOP5 (adds A*) 0.00 (BFS 63, DFS 36). The banned BFS/DFS is re-implemented, 41-92% of the time without naming it ('backtracking'). FRLP-implied avoidance foresaw the failure (<1e-8 for TOP, SCATTER and TOP5) but not ARC (0.9995 vs 0.37). Ban prompts raise the likelihood of the banned programs relative to neutral twins (priming). Landing rank tests are degenerate (survivor mass only in ARC and SCATTER; exact permutation p >= 0.33 per set, >= 0.11 pooled). On ARC the ladder-type forecasts hit DFS (+1.06 nats/program over uniform) and PROXIMITY missed. T2 leave-one-survivor: 'will' Dijkstra 0.95, BF/SPFA 0.51, BiDijkstra/Floyd/Dial 0.00, despite 'can' 0.35/1.0/0.9 when forced. Geometry: centroid MDS stress-1 0.025/0.006; neighbours BFS-BiBFS, DFS-IDDFS, A*-greedy; no persistent H1 loop. Within-ban probes at g16 are near chance on T1 and 0.69 balanced accuracy on T2 LOS(BF/SPFA) (p=0.03); 'pre' probes are a position artefact. POST HOC (not pre-registered): the BFS/DFS mix is the same across the four multi-bans (BFS share 0.63-0.72, chi-square p=0.48) whether or not DFS is on the list. A long ban list therefore acts like a generic perturbation of the default (about 1/3 DFS) rather than a list-specific veto, while a single BFS ban moves the model to 86% DFS. Every method_out example carries predict_default (no change) and predict_ladder. Multi-ban rows also carry the committed top-1 forecasts: on multi-bans the default is right 64% of the time versus at most 10% for any survivor forecast. Caveats: FRLP ESS < 5 in 97% of cells; bf16 scoring noise of about 1 nat per program; T2 sum exp(L) is 0.17.
