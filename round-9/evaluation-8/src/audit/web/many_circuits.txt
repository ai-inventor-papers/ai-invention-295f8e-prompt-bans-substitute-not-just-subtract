> Abstract:Circuit discovery methods identify subgraphs that explain model behaviors, and structural differences between discovered circuits are commonly interpreted as evidence of distinct mechanisms. We test this assumption by varying input-token frequency while holding the task fixed. The discovered circuits appear specialized by frequency when compared structurally, but functional and representational analyses show no reliable evidence of corresponding differences. We term this mismatch phantom specialization. Using the Literal Sequence Copying task across four frequency bands plus a frequency-weighted control, we extract 75 circuits from five Pythia models (70M-1.4B).
