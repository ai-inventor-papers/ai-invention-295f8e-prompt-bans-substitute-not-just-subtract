## Layman Summary

We sampled 5,220 programs from an AI model at different randomness settings and prompt seeds and checked whether it picks a different algorithm or just rewords the same one.

## Full Summary

Screen on Qwen3-8B (vLLM 0.11.0, RTX 5090) over T1 (unweighted grid path search, any valid path) and T2 (weighted directed shortest path). 5,220 generations, each labelled by hidden tests + runtime tracer + AST rules + blind judge: A0 no-cue baseline (N=100/task); A1 temperature grid T in {0.3,0.7,1.0,1.3} x {top-k=10, top-p=0.95}, thinking off, N=100/cell; A6 the same grid thinking on, N=100/cell; entropy/word/semantic seeds; four creativity instructions; forced implementations; gen-then-select. FINDINGS: (1) Temperature and sampler do not change the algorithm. BFS share on T1 is 797/800 thinking off (every cell >= 0.99) and 800/800 thinking on; Dijkstra share on T2 is 797/800 off and 800/800 on. (2) Phrasings grow with T while ideas stay at about 1: on T1 at theta=0.8, phrasing clusters go from 3-4 at T=0.3 to 24-36 at T=1.3; log PPI slope about +1.2 per unit T, paired CI > 0. T2 shows a smaller rise (1-2 to 3-6 clusters; top-p CI includes 0). (3) Random seeds shift behaviour by <= 1 pp (90% CI within +-15 pp). At the read position, the entropy seed stays within paraphrase noise (displacement ratio 0.73 T1 / 1.16 T2), but the word seed on T1 moves the mode subspace 1.56x (CI 1.27-1.87), close to a neutral insert (0.81/1.29). The semantic seed moves R 3.4-3.7x; on T2 it shifts behaviour 66 pp toward Bellman-Ford (p=0.0004), on T1 only +2 pp toward bidirectional BFS (p=0.08). (4) 'Fully novel' and 'list 10 then implement' cut T2 Dijkstra by 15 and 11 pp. Gen-then-select reaches DA_idea@25 3.98/3.00 vs 1.26/1.00 for A0, at pass rates of 55%/75%. (5) Variance of trajectory states (first 64 generated tokens): complement variance rises steeply with T; the raw mode-subspace share is flat on T1 and rises slightly on T2; the excess over a data-matched null rises, so P7 is missed. (6) All thinking traces hit the 2,048-token cap; the first approach mentioned is BFS/Dijkstra in >= 98% of traces. Mechanical verdict: MIXED (S-T6 variance-share slope and random-seed equivalence at R fail). Hash-committed forecast: 6/8 hits.
