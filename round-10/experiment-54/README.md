## Full Summary

NOV-R experiment (plan gen_plan_experiment_4_idx4), 1,143 one-shot online bin-packing priority functions in the exact FunSearch interface from Qwen3-8B (no thinking, local vLLM FP8, N 50 per cell), Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-32B (thinking, OpenRouter DeepInfra fp8, N 30 per cell after the reduction ladder). Cells: BASE, cumulative name bans of each model's own top-k textbook rules (NAME_1..5, templates W1-W3), a described ban (DESC_5), held-out templates (HOLD_5), and two novelty instructions (INSTR_NEW, INSTR_LONG). Baseline instrument = lexical compliance L1 (no banned name or abbreviation in the program); proposed instrument = mechanistic compliance (exact-decision agreement < 0.95 with every banned rule on 2,000 committed probe states, plus a stochastic random-fit matcher), gated beforehand on planted controls (min margin 0.49).
